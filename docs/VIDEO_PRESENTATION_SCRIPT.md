## 📝 Scene-by-Scene Script

---

### Scene 1: Introduction & Authentication (0:00 - 0:20)
**Visual:**  
*Screen shows the sleek dark-mode DataCrew Login Screen on [datacrew-gamma.vercel.app](https://datacrew-gamma.vercel.app).*

**Voiceover:**  
> "Welcome to DataCrew — the autonomous multi-agent platform that turns dirty, unorganized data into verified, business-ready intelligence. Today, we're taking a 3-minute walkthrough from sign-in to governance settings."

---

### Scene 2: Datasets Hub & File Ingestion (0:20 - 0:45)
**Visual:**  
*Cursor clicks **Sign In**, opening the `/datasets` grid. Cursor clicks **Upload File** and selects a raw CSV dataset (`dirty_customers.csv`). The health score gauge immediately evaluates the dataset at 68/100.*

**Voiceover:**  
> "Upon entering the workspace, DataCrew's ingestion engine automatically computes a real-time Health Score based on completeness, format consistency, and duplicates. You can upload CSV, Excel, or Parquet files, or connect directly via API endpoints."

---

### Scene 3: AI Data Cleaning & Version Timeline (0:45 - 1:15)
**Visual:**  
*Screen switches to `/datasets/[id]/clean`. Show the Before & After Health Score Gauge. The AI cleaning plan highlights steps: `Fill Missing Values`, `Standardize Dates`, and `Normalize Phone Numbers`. Cursor clicks **Apply Cleaning Plan** — the score jumps from 68 to 94/100. Then navigate to the `/data` tab to view the live paginated table and version timeline.*

**Voiceover:**  
> "In the Clean workspace, DataCrew's AI agent analyzes the dataset and generates a deterministic cleaning plan. With one click, missing fields are imputed, dates are standardized, and format errors disappear — boosting data quality from 68 to 94. Over in the Data tab, you can inspect the updated dataset or instantly roll back to any prior version."

---

### Scene 4: High-Speed Deduplication & Risk Matrix (1:15 - 1:40)
**Visual:**  
*Navigate to `/dedupe` tab. Show the fuzzy variant cluster (`"Hyd"`, `"hyderabad"`, `"Hydrabad"` grouped together). Click **Merge Cluster**. Next, click `/risks` tab to show the DPDP Compliance Risk Matrix and red PII tags (Aadhaar, Phone, Email).*

**Voiceover:**  
> "Our high-speed Deduplication engine groups near-duplicate records using prefix-bucketed fuzzy logic, allowing you to merge variant entries in milliseconds. In the Privacy tab, DataCrew automatically scans for sensitive PII like Aadhaar or phone numbers, enforcing India's DPDP Act compliance out of the box."

---

### Scene 5: Ask AI, Time-Series Forecasting & Strategy Hub (1:40 - 2:10)
**Visual:**  
*Click `/ask` tab. Type: `"What are top 5 customer regions by revenue?"` Show the instant answer and transparent SQL audit log. Click `/forecast` to show confidence intervals. Click `/strategy` to highlight ranked Rupee (₹) commercial opportunities.*

**Voiceover:**  
> "With Ask AI, team members can query datasets in plain English, backed by transparent SQL audit trails. The Forecast tab projects future trends with 80% confidence intervals, while the Strategy Hub translates clean data into ranked commercial opportunities."

### Scene 6: Autonomous Agent Crew & Data Passports (2:10 - 2:35)

## 💡 Speaker Tips for Video Recording
1. **Screen Resolution:** Record at `1920x1080` (1080p) or `4K` with browser zoom set to `100%`.
2. **Pacing:** Maintain a steady, confident speaking pace (~140 words per minute).
3. **Cursor Movement:** Move the cursor smoothly without sudden jerks when switching between tabs.
